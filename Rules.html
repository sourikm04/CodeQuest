Rules & Eligibility

Eligibility Criteria

  • The hackathon is open to participants of all skill levels
  • Participants must be 18 years or older
  • Teams can consist of 1 to 4 members
  • Individual registration required

Project Requirements

  • Projects must be built from scratch during the event
  • Must align with announced theme
  • Complete submission before deadline

Code of Conduct

  • Show respect to all participants
  • Collaboration encouraged, no plagiarism
  • No offensive content allowed
